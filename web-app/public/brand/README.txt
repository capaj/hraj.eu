Objevte hry ve svém okolí, přidejte se k týmu nebo uspořádejte vlastní akci.
Méně plánování, více hraní — a noví přátelé k tomu.

LOGOS / LOGA

SVGs are the source artwork, with the lettering converted to paths. They do
not need external fonts and can be scaled without losing quality.
Logo PNGs are 6× exports with transparent backgrounds; the mark is 1024 × 1024.
The wordmark uses outlined Lato Heavy lettering. The symbol is a soccer ball
with classic pentagonal panels on a green tile: a shared love of the game.

USAGE / POUŽITÍ

Leave at least half the mark's height as clear space around the logo.
Keep the supplied proportions and colors. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor,
or add shadows or other effects. Choose a background with good contrast.
Use the standalone mark when the full logo would be narrower than 120 px.
Write the name as hraj.eu, in lowercase, and link to https://hraj.eu when possible.
These assets may be used to identify hraj.eu in articles, listings, and
community materials. Do not imply endorsement or an official partnership.

Kolem loga ponechte volný prostor alespoň o polovině výšky symbolu.
